What is Hoodie?

A trading team on Robinhood Chain that answers to you.

Ask what is moving, or say what to buy, in plain words. Hoodie researches it and brings you a trade to approve.

ShieldBot checks every token before money goes in: the creator, the holders, split buys, honeypots.

Autopilot trades only inside the budget you set, from a wallet only you can withdraw from.
